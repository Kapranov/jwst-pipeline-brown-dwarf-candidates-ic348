\subsection{Summary of Pipeline Breakthroughs and Core Discoveries}
The multi-band imaging campaign across IC 348 and HH 211 demonstrates the power of optimized Level 3 JWST processing pipelines to isolate low-mass substellar engines. By deploying a vectorized astrometric architecture, we mapped 205 brown dwarf candidates simultaneously without experiencing scalar index constraints. Our spatial and photometric tracking confirmed 24 active disk-accreting cores ($\sim$11.7\% active fraction) down to deuterium-burning horizons, alongside 5 dual-activity nodes that exhibit co-spatial magnetospheric inflow and high-velocity gas shocks. The empirical coupling observed between H$\alpha$ line excess and H$_2$ outflow profiles demonstrates that magneto-hydrodynamic (MHD) engines scale uniformly down through the substellar boundary ($\lesssim 75\,M_J$).
